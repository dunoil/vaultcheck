The problem
VaultCheck · Terminal 3

Eligibility checks have a privacy problem

A normal eligibility check asks for

Shared in plain form, copied, stored, and kept far longer than the check itself.

✕ Your full identity document
✕ Your exact monthly income
✕ Your home address
✕ Your date of birth

All of it, just to answer a yes or no question.
